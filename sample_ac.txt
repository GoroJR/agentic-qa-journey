User Story: Login with email and password

Acceptance Criteria:
- User can log in with a valid email and password
- Email field must be a valid email format
- Password must be at least 8 characters
- After 5 failed attempts, account is locked for 15 minutes
- Successful login redirects to /dashboard
- Failed login shows error message without revealing which field was wrongUser Story: Login with email and password
